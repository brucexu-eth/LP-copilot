# LP lifecycle

Updated 2026-09-11. The current managed-LP contract is defined in [PRD](../PRD.md), [technical design](../TECHNICAL_DESIGN.md) and [acceptance](ACCEPTANCE.md).

Entry approval and a bounded, revocable Privy management mandate replace the previous mandatory per-transaction approval for every subsequent action. Single-sided range orders are out of scope. No credentials, funds or deployment are implied by this specification.
