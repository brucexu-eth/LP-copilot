What this teaches

A range changes your inventory. As WETH becomes more expensive, this pool converts your active liquidity toward USDC. As WETH becomes cheaper, it converts toward WETH.

Out of range does not mean liquidated. Your active liquidity becomes one-sided and stops earning swap fees until the price re-enters the range. Its value can still change.

Exiting is not the same as selling. EXIT holds the withdrawn token mix. WIDEN keeps unused tokens in your wallet. Neither is a free operation in real life.
